Scope: whole multi-page site of Norte Estudio (home, project pages, studio, contact). Mode: Experience (portfolio of work) with a Persuade close (contact).
Audience: prospective clients; portfolio reviewers. Action: browse projects, open one, send an enquiry.
Constraints: Astro static, native view transitions, CSP, WCAG AA, reduced motion.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the studio's monograph: a quiet architectural publication where each building is shown big, labeled with plain facts, and the interface behaves like turning pages. It refuses the category default of a dark full-screen slider with oversized thin titles and a hamburger menu.

OWN-WORLD: Lime white (#f5f3ef) ground, graphite text (#1c1b19), stone hairlines (#cfc8bd), one brick accent (#9c4f33) used only for state (current page, hover, focus). Schibsted Grotesk at moderate sizes with tight tracking for heads and tabular figures for facts. Photographs full-bleed or on a strict 12-column grid, square corners, no shadows, no cards. Captions and facts set like a monograph's plate notes.

STORY: The visitor reads the studio's stance in one line, sees a building at full width, scans featured projects and the complete index, opens a project and reads its facts, then contacts the studio.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with the "Norte" wordmark and three links. Below, a two-line statement at about 4rem across 8 columns, a one-line subtitle and a "Ver proyectos" link. The first project photograph starts in the lower third and grows to full bleed as the visitor scrolls.

FORM: Agreed direction from the approved plan (editorial and sober), adjusted to the user's recorded taste; no concept-seed roll, because the brief is pinned. Signature interaction: a native cross-document view transition. The project photograph on the index morphs into the hero of the project page, and back. Motion grammar: scroll-driven reveals (image scale-in, section fade-up) and page morphs only, no loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
