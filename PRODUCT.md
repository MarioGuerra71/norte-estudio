# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7 (static, multi-page), native cross-document View Transitions, CSS scroll-driven animations. Vercel free plan. Self-hosted font, no third-party scripts.

## Users

Private clients and public bodies looking for an architect in Andalucía, browsing the studio's work before getting in touch. The real audience is reviewers of Mario Guerra's portfolio: this piece shows multi-page structure, editorial layout and native browser motion.

## Product Purpose

Website for «Norte Estudio», a fictitious architecture studio in Sevilla. Success: a visitor browses projects, opens one, understands its facts and reasoning, and sends an enquiry through the contact form (simulated).

## Positioning

A small studio that works with light, local materials (lime, brick, stone) and reuse of existing buildings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home with a statement, featured projects, a full project index, the approach, and a contact call.
- One static page per project: facts (location, year, area, programme, status), text, gallery, previous/next.
- Studio page (team as names and roles, no invented awards) and contact page with a validated, simulated form.
- Native View Transitions between project list and project page; scroll-driven reveals; everything works with reduced motion and without JS.
- Strict CSP (hashed), WCAG 2.2 AA, Lighthouse 100 in a11y, best practices and SEO. Zero cost.

## Brand Commitments

- Fictitious studio, visible "Proyecto de demostración" notice; projects, people and figures are invented, and photos are Unsplash stock of unrelated buildings.
- Spanish copy.
- User taste (2026-10-19): elegant and restrained; moderate headline scale; no loud colors. Agreed direction for this site: editorial and sober.

## Evidence on Hand

- Unsplash photos, credited per project and in the footer.
- No real clients, awards, press or budgets. Do not invent awards or testimonials.

## Product Principles

- The buildings lead; the interface steps back.
- Facts before adjectives.
- Native platform first: links, pages, browser transitions.
- Honest demo.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA; reduced motion disables view transitions and reveals; every image has a descriptive alt.
